## English

**Understand videos, even when the language is unfamiliar.**

Rawya is a free, open source macOS player that can generate subtitles from speech and translate them into a language you understand. Completed subtitles appear while the rest of the video is still being processed.

- Generate subtitles automatically when a video has none.
- Translate generated subtitles into your preferred language.
- Start reading completed subtitles without waiting for the entire video.
- Process audio locally with Apple AI on supported Macs.
- Play common media formats and manage subtitles, playlists, chapters, picture-in-picture, and playback history.

**Requirements:** AI subtitles require macOS 26 or later, with language availability determined by macOS. Basic playback requires macOS 12 or later on Apple silicon, or macOS 10.15 or later on Intel Macs.

Open the DMG and drag Rawya into Applications. Rawya checks for stable updates daily and also supports manual update checks from the application menu.
